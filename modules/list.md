# Official Dungeons & Dragons / AD&D Adventure Modules

A catalog of official **coded** adventure modules (the "B2"-style codes) published by TSR (and where noted, later WotC) for *Dungeons & Dragons* (Basic/BECMI line) and *Advanced Dungeons & Dragons* (1st & 2nd edition). Scope: numbered/lettered **adventure** modules — accessories, sourcebooks, boxed campaign settings, and rulebooks are excluded except where they are primarily adventures.

Line key: **D&D** = Basic/Expert/Companion/Master/Immortals (BECMI) · **1e** = AD&D 1st ed · **2e** = AD&D 2nd ed.

Dates are first print year (best-effort; to be verified by sub-agents).
